Welcome to UpackX

Lightweight native archive extractor for macOS.
Optimized for macOS 14+ • Intel x86_64

This installer will guide you through installing UpackX on your Mac.

Key Highlights:

  • Native & Lightweight: Built purely with Swift and AppKit. Minimal memory footprint (~20 MB RSS).
  • Seamless Finder Integration: Access "Giải nén nhanh với UpackX" and "Giải nén… với UpackX" directly from your right-click context menu.
  • Zero Daemons: No background processes running when inactive.
  • Safe Extraction: No unwanted enclosing folders, safe directory merging, and automatic collision protection.
  • Broad Format Support: ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, and multipart volume archives (.part1.rar, .7z.001).
Post-install Tip: Launch UpackX once from your Applications folder to verify and enable Finder Context Menu Services in your user session.
